### Clinical Researcher, Research and Clinical Development, Wysa
*Responsibilities*: Worked as a researcher with Wysa, an AI conversational agent for mental health support. Tested
efficacy and usage using mixed-method approaches and RCTs. Generated real-world evidence of digital mental health intervention efficacy by analyzing multi-modal data from 1M+ users, using logistic regressions, K-Means clustering, and other statistical methods (ANOVAs, t-tests, other inferential statistics). Used statistical power analysis and effect size calculations to optimize RCTs. Identified multimodal distress patterns across 5M+ global crisis conversations, informing AI model safety protocols and reducing inappropriate responses. Conducted R programming training sessions for the team.

*Learning Outcomes*: Learned query writing and database management using MongoDB Atlas, Developed analytical
skills, Research design, Testing efficacy, Mixed-method approaches, Qualitative and quantitative data, Academic re-
search paper writing, R programming, Scientific writing, Presentation skills, Communication skills, Time management
skills, Independent work, Teamwork, and Teaching.
