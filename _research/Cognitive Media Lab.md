### Research Associate, [Cognitive Media Lab](https://cognitivemedialab.ucsd.edu/)

#### Master's Thesis: Examining and Profiling Individual Differences among Social Media Users based on Misinformation Engagement Behaviors
*Status*: Submitting for publication. [Thesis repository](https://github.com/dyuthiii/Misinfo-Thesis)

Used multiple regression models to analyze user interactions with 36 COVID-19 misinformation tweets and identify key drivers of misinformation spread (R² = 0.45).

Conducted Silhouette Analysis and K-Means Clustering to segment 800+ users into 3 personas based on passive and active misinformation engagement patterns, revealing potential high-risk amplifier groups.

#### Source Trustworthiness in Abortion Pill Marketing
Conducting Conjoint Analysis and Cluster Analysis of abortion pill marketing on social media, studying the interaction effects between variables that affect source trustworthiness.

#### LLM Performance in Pharmaceutical Advice
Co-authored a manuscript comparing LLM performance to pharmacists, nurses, and practitioners, contributing to the background, introduction, and results sections. See [Publications](/publications/).

*Learning Outcomes*: Multiple Regression, Clustering, Conjoint Analysis, Web Scraping, Python, R, Manuscript Writing
