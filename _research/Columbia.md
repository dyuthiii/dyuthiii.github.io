### Data Scientist/ML Engineer: LLM-Powered Mental Health Support Tool, Columbia University Practicum
**January 2025 - May 2025**

Designed an end-to-end workflow for a HIPAA-compliant mental health chatbot, incorporating informed consent, CBT-based interventions, and crisis escalation protocols aligned with clinical best practices. Contributed to building a prototype using Streamlit.

Established 10 safety and relevance metrics for AI mental health systems, including response appropriateness, risk detection accuracy, therapeutic alliance measures, and clinical datasets for model training.

*Learning Outcomes*: Python, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, AI Safety Evaluation

### Research Assistant, Political Science Department, Columbia University
*Project*: Studied the influence of bot utilization by politicians on social media, and its effects on financial contributions and political outcomes.

*Role*: Web scraping for politicians and their contributions using an API; data analysis.

*Learning Outcomes*: Web Scraping, Python, Data Analysis, SQL

### Ethics & Usage Lead at GradeMate, Columbia University
*Project*: GradeMate is an AI-integrated approach to support instructors in evaluating student assignments. The app leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to read through assignments and provide feedback to students.

*Role*: Studied ethical considerations and developed guardrails for our model. My role involved literature review, LLM performance analysis, statistical analysis, and a systematic internal study to evaluate bias (using an API to study bias in grading based on race, ethnicity, gender, and nationality). I also outlined a UX research study for when the app is safe and ready for testing.

*Learning Outcomes*: Statistical Analysis of LLM Performance, AI Ethics Research, Research Design, UX Research.
