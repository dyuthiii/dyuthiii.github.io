### Research Assistant, [Emotion and Cognition Lab](https://cns.iisc.ac.in/srikanth/), Centre for Neuroscience
Working with Prof. Srikanth Padmala to study the neural mechanisms of emotion-cognition interactions, using computational modeling on cognitive behavioral paradigms in decision-making.

*Project*: Implementing Drift Diffusion Modeling (DDM) to evaluate the cognitive mechanisms underlying decision-making under high vs. low perceptual load.

*Learning Outcomes*: Computational Modeling, Drift Diffusion Modeling, Cognitive Behavioral Paradigms
